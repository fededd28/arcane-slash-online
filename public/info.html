What it's about

You spawn in a closed arena with other warlocks. Everyone fights everyone. No teams, no final round: you fight, earn points and try to reach first place.

  1. Fight. Cast spells from a distance and use the sword when someone gets close. Every kill earns points, more if the rival was in the lead.
  2. Collect orbs. They appear all over the map and give points. Big ones are worth five times more.
  3. Reach #1. The leader has more health and more mana, but everyone hunts them.
  4. Fall and return. You respawn after 5 seconds. When few players are online, bots fill the room.

The four spells

Each one costs mana. A spell that misses costs nothing.

Petrify

Key 1 · 60 mana

14 damage and freezes the rival in place for 6 seconds. Perfect for finishing with the sword.

Spectral Bolt

Key 2 · 50 mana

64 damage. The cheapest and safest way to fight at range.

Cataclysm

Key 3 · 180 mana

157 damage. Expensive, but it takes about half of a rival's health.

Dispel

Key 4 · 100 mana

Removes negative effects such as petrification and gives you back your movement.
